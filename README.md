## Make every grind count

From your first skill milestone to your next collection-log slot, choose the targets that matter to you.

| Track | Set your sights on |
| --- | --- |
| **Levels & XP** | Skill levels, session XP, total level and XP, combat level, and base level and XP |
| **Bosses & activities** | Kill counts, raid and minigame completions, Slayer points and task streaks |
| **Quests** | Individual quests and quest series |
| **Achievement Diary** | Diary regions and tiers |
| **Combat Achievements** | Boss tasks, difficulties and reward tiers |
| **Collection log** | Total unlock targets, individual unlocks or entire pages, with item previews and obtained progress |
| **Items** | Supplies and equipment across known inventory, equipment, bank and supported storage |
| **Personal milestones** | Custom counters, notes, checklists and linked milestones |

Before adding collection-log milestones, open your in-game collection log. Grindstone captures loaded unlocks automatically and verifies them against the game’s total. Check **Snapshots** on the dashboard; hover the information buttons for capture status and refresh tips. Refresh the snapshot after playing on another device. Use **Add all collection-log pages** to add missing page milestones.

## Start your next milestone

1. Enable **Grindstone** in RuneLite, log in and open its sidebar.
2. Select **+ Add milestone**, choose what to track and set your target.
3. Review, save and play. Supported milestones update as you make progress.

<img src="docs/images/add-goal.png" width="464" alt="Add milestone dialog with six focus groups, searchable skills, target presets and a Slayer level 99 target">

Played on mobile or another computer? Your tracked game progress refreshes when you return to RuneLite. **Since last seen** shows gains compared with a previous saved reading. Open the collection log or relevant activity interface to load missing data. Exact completion times and progress across routine resets cannot be reconstructed.

Start with your own milestones or choose a preset pack from the **…** menu. Customize your sidebar, tracking preferences and alerts in **RuneLite settings → Grindstone**.

### Right-click to get started faster

Add a milestone straight from the game interface with the subject already selected.

| Right-click | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| A skill or Total level | **Set level milestone**, **Set XP milestone**, or a total/base target |
| Combat level in Combat Options | **Set combat milestone** |
| A quest | **Track quest** |
| An achievement diary region | **Track Easy/Medium/Hard/Elite diary** |
| A collection-log item or page | **Track unlock** or **Track page** |
| A Combat Achievement task | **Track task** |
| A Combat Achievements boss entry | **Track boss achievements** |
| A supported boss NPC | **Track kills** |

Choose your target and review it before saving. Each shortcut can be switched off in settings.

## Keep your priorities in focus

- **Put your next milestone first.** Pin milestones and group them by category or skill.
- **Find what matters now.** Search your milestones, filter the list or select a dashboard status counter.
- **Build a routine.** Create daily, weekly or monthly routines and use **Routines** to see recurring progress and upcoming resets.
- **Plan ahead.** Add personal deadlines and optional reminders in the sidebar.
- **Celebrate the finish.** Choose completion popups, local chat messages or optional desktop notifications, then revisit your milestones in **History**.
- **Remember your routines.** Enable daily, weekly or monthly reminders in Notifications. Choose chat or desktop alerts, the interval, login and reset reminders, numbers, countdowns and chat colours.
- **Manage your milestones together.** Use bulk actions to organize several milestones, and export a profile backup to keep a local copy.

## Track progress beside the game

Open **… → Progress overlay** to follow **Daily routines**, **All routines**, **Milestones**, **Pinned**, or **All tracked** while the sidebar is closed. Progress bars show what is left, completed routines stay checked off, and daily routines show their next reset.

Hold **Alt** and drag the tracker to move it. Adjust its width, entry limit, progress bars and completed routines in **RuneLite settings → Grindstone → Progress overlay**.

Completion popups celebrate newly reached targets with a compact card, a completion badge and a gentle fade. Targets reached together share one notification.

## Stay on top of routines

In **Grindstone → Notifications**, switch on **Enable routine reminders** and choose daily, weekly or monthly reminders, their interval, and local chat or desktop notifications. Reminders list unfinished tracked routines with exact progress and time until reset. Completed routines are skipped.

Completion chat messages show exact progress, such as **1,250 / 1,000**. Colour and completion numbers can each be switched off.
